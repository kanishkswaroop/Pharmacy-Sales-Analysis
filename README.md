🚀 Power BI Case Study | Pharma Sales & Performance Analysis|

For this challenge, I created a 3‑page Power BI report analyzing a European pharmacy distributor operating across multiple countries.

Objective:
The goal was to create a report that clearly shows how sales and profitability differ across countries, regions, and individual pharmacies, how product categories and brands perform by location, and how each region contributes to overall business results.

What the dashboard covers
> Shows overall performance for revenue, cost, margin, and units sold
> Compares results across months and years
> Breaks down sales and profitability by country, region, city, and pharmacy
> Highlights how products, categories, and brands perform with and without promotions

Key business questions answered
> How sales, costs, margins, and units sold are performing compared to last year
> Which countries, regions, cities, and pharmacies are performing best or worst
> Which products, categories, and brands drive the most sales and profit
> How promotions affect revenue and margin
> How performance changes month by month across the business
